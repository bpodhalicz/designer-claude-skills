## Why this shape works

- The **snapshot** lets the person decide in ten seconds whether it's safe to repeat.
- **Verified claims carry no inline chip** — only the shaky ones do, so the eye goes straight to what needs care.
- The **sources table** is scannable; each row says what it actually backs and how solid it is.
- The **log** is a receipt, including the failed searches — proof the gap is a real gap, not laziness.
- The **inferred** claim is labeled *and* attributed to the facts it's built on, so it's never mistaken for a citation.
